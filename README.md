**Problem:**

_Final:_
The convenience of having social media right in our pockets has made face-to-face connections increasingly difficult. From our personal experience, it’s rare to meet like-minded individuals platonically and romantically. Certain niches often host their own organized events or gatherings to foster connection, such as sports teams, clubs, and societies. Music is an interest that most individuals share, and yet is not associated with dedicated, low-commitment casual events to bring people together who enjoy the same genres. For instance, there are no opportunities for people who love pop in a casual venue, far from a bustling nightlife. Without others to attend concerts and music venues with, many end up in the circumstance of going alone.
