## Problem

The convenience of having social media right in our pockets has made face-to-face connections increasingly difficult. From our personal experience, it’s rare to meet like-minded individuals platonically and romantically. Certain niches often host their own organized events or gatherings to foster connection, such as sports teams, clubs, and societies. These methods of traditional socialization rely significantly on structured commitment and often require high financial investments, rigid scheduling, or skill proficiency. Music is an interest that most individuals share, and yet is not associated with dedicated, low-commitment casual events to bring people together who enjoy the same genres.

For instance, there’s no opportunities for people who love pop in a casual venue, far from a bustling nightlife. Informal public gathering spaces, separate from home and work, contribute to the foundation of community life. Without others to attend concerts and music venues with, many end up in the circumstance of going alone. While live music events provide peak social experiences, going alone may induce social anxiety, and the lack of having “concert buddies” deters many individuals from attending live events altogether. Consider your average, commonly pedestrian who defaults to isolated headphone listening, rather than shared community experiences. Every one of us may be some music fan that the other would never be aware of unless there was some service that could exactly bridge that gap we have. In fact, widespread social disconnection poses significant physical health risks, equivalent to smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day (Office of the Surgeon General, 2023). Younger demographics are reported to have the highest rates of isolation, as well as a dramatic decline in time spent face-to-face with peers. According to studies conducted separately by Harvey (2020) and Tarr et al. (2014), shared music experiences trigger the release of important neurochemicals, such as endogenous endorphins and oxytocin, which facilitate social cohesion as well as interpersonal trust.

## Solution

Foundational specifications for our solution include:

* Features similar to Spotify where the user can stream songs: this would include libraries of music genres and artists to select and discover from. The streaming format will resemble that of a swipe UI seen on the Instagram reels platform and dating apps like Hinge, Grindr, etc.
* Swiping to the left will skip the preview of the song, while swiping to the right will allow the user to listen to the full version. Each song would essentially have their own swipe card, complete with the album cover, playtime, supporting coloured background, and “like” button.
* A series of tags will be associated with each “song card” which will identify the user’s preferences that will allow for songs that resemble their style and tastes to be recommended. This will also streamline the matching algorithm - whereby users are automatically matched with each other if they share several similar tags and liked songs.
* After being matched, the users will have a chance to review their musical compatibility to decide if they would like to proceed with the match. If both parties agree, then a chat box is opened and the users are free to interact with each other and exchange contact information.
* Verification/user/personal information safety features:
    * Users would have to verify their email that they register their account with. Otherwise, an error pop-up would appear and the user will not be allowed to interact with any of the app’s features except for the login and account creation.
    * Each user, upon the creation of their account, might share personal information that allows socialization between users to expand onto other platforms (e.g., phone number, WhatsApp, Instagram handles, DIscord usernames, etc.). What the user chooses to share is up to their discretion, but this is for their own benefit in order to make new friends and relationships from this application. Encryption features can be implemented to safeguard personal contact information and exchanges in the chat box.
